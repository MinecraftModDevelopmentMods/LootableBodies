2.4.2.111021 - Minecraft 1.11.2, Forge

- Fixed item loss and duplication during death, corpse inventory transfers,
  hotbar swaps, dragging and cancelled drops.
- Added protection for corpses when upgrading 1.10.2 and 1.11.2 worlds,
  including bodies in chunks you visit later.
- Kept unreadable item records for recovery when missing mods return.
- Corpses wait for unloaded terrain and only move out of walls when there is
  enough clear, loaded space for the whole body.
- Added a Config button on the Mods list. Appearance changes apply straight
  away; world and server rules take effect after restarting Minecraft.
- Fixed untranslated names on nameless corpses and their spawn eggs.

Existing settings and defaults are unchanged. Back up your world before
upgrading, including its data folder. Downgrading an upgraded world is unsupported.
